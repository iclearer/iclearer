Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what iClearer collects, why we collect it, and how you can control it. The website is at iclearer.com.

1. What we collect

  • Account: email address, license key, plan, number of Mac seats, and status.
  • Device seat: a hashed hardware identifier for each Mac you activate (not your raw serial number), so we can enforce your seat limit and restore your plan.
  • Optional marketing opt-in: whether you asked to hear about updates and new products.
  • Payments: handled by Creem (Merchant of Record). We receive what we need to fulfill your order (for example email, plan, and payment status). We do not store full card numbers.
  • Support and feedback: name, email, and message if you contact us or leave feedback.
  • Website basics: standard server or hosting logs (such as IP address and pages requested) that may be collected by our host.

2. What stays on your Mac

Scan results, file names, folder paths, and cleanup history stay on your Mac. We do not upload your files or scan contents to our servers.

3. How we use data

  • Create and manage your license
  • Send required emails (license key, receipts, security notices)
  • Enforce Mac seat limits
  • Provide support
  • Send product updates only if you opt in
  • Improve reliability and prevent abuse

4. Legal bases

Where applicable (for example under GDPR), we process data to perform our contract with you (providing the app and license), to meet legal obligations, and—with your consent—for optional marketing.

5. Sharing

We share data only with services that help us run iClearer, such as:

  • Hosting and database (for example Supabase)
  • Email delivery (for license and notices)
  • Payments (Creem, as Merchant of Record)

We do not sell your personal data.

6. Retention

We keep account and license records while your account is active and as needed for support, fraud prevention, and legal requirements. You can ask us to delete account data where the law allows; we may retain limited records required for accounting or disputes.

7. Security

We use industry-standard protections for license data, including hashed device identifiers and signed license blobs used by the Mac app for offline grace. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Your choices

  • Opt out of marketing anytime (unsubscribe link or email us)
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your account data by emailing us
  • Stop using the app and delete it from your Mac at any time

9. Children

iClearer is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.

10. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the EU, US, or other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards offered by those providers.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.

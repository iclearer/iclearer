Privacy

iClearer stores your email, optional marketing opt-in, plan, license key, and a hashed device id so we can restore your plan on a new Mac. Scan data stays on your Mac.

Transactional messages (license, receipts, security) are required. Marketing messages are sent only if you opt in, and you can unsubscribe anytime in Settings.

Questions: support@iclearer.com

How to delete old iPhone backups on Mac

Updated 12 November 2026 · By the iClearer team

Short answer

To delete old iPhone backups on a Mac, open System Settings → General → Storage, click the i next to iOS Files, select the backup and click Delete. With the iPhone connected, you can also open it in Finder and choose Manage Backups. Backups are stored in ~/Library/Application Support/MobileSync/Backup. Deleting a backup removes nothing from the iPhone itself.

1. Delete from Storage settings

This works without the device and shows the size of each backup.

  1. Open the Apple menu → System Settings → General → Storage.
  2. Click the i next to iOS Files. If you do not see iOS Files, there are no device backups on this Mac.
  3. Select a backup. The list shows the device name, the date and the size.
  4. Click Delete and confirm.

2. Delete from Finder with the device connected

  1. Connect the iPhone or iPad to your Mac with a cable.
  2. Open Finder and select the device in the sidebar.
  3. In the General tab, click Manage Backups.
  4. Select a backup, click Delete Backup and confirm.

On macOS Mojave or earlier, backups are managed in iTunes instead: iTunes → Preferences → Devices.

3. Delete from the backup folder

  1. In Finder, press Shift Command G.
  2. Enter ~/Library/Application Support/MobileSync/Backup and press Return.
  3. Each folder with a long name of letters and numbers is one backup. Press Command J and tick Calculate All Sizes to see how big each one is.
  4. Move the ones you do not need to the Trash, then empty the Trash.

Note: folder names do not tell you which device a backup belongs to. Use the Storage settings or Finder method if you have backups of more than one device.

Which backups to keep

  • Keep the most recent backup of every device you still use, unless that device also backs up to iCloud.
  • Safe to delete: backups of devices you sold or replaced, and older backups of a device that has a newer one.
  • A backup cannot be rebuilt once it is gone. If you are not sure, copy the folder to an external drive first.

iCloud backups are separate. They are stored by Apple, not on your Mac, and deleting a backup on your Mac does not affect them.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to delete old iPhone backups on a Mac?

Yes, as long as you keep the most recent backup of any device you still use, or that device backs up to iCloud. Deleting a backup removes only the copy on your Mac. Nothing on the iPhone changes.

Where are iPhone backups stored on a Mac?

In ~/Library/Application Support/MobileSync/Backup. Each backup is a folder with a long name made of letters and numbers. In Finder, press Shift-Command-G and enter that path to open it.

How much space do iPhone backups take on a Mac?

Roughly as much as the data on the device, so often tens of gigabytes each. System Settings → General → Storage → iOS Files shows the exact size of every backup.

Does deleting a backup on my Mac delete my iCloud backup?

No. iCloud backups are stored by Apple and are managed separately on the device, under Settings → your name → iCloud. Backups on your Mac and backups in iCloud do not affect each other.

Why can I not see iOS Files in Storage settings?

iOS Files only appears when the Mac has at least one device backup or downloaded iOS software update. If it is missing, there are no backups stored on this Mac.

Refund Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Refund Policy explains how refunds and cancellations work for iClearer paid plans. Payments are processed by Paddle, our Merchant of Record.

1. 14-day money-back guarantee

If you buy a paid iClearer plan (monthly, yearly, or one-time) and you are not satisfied, you can request a full refund within 14 days of the purchase date.

2. How to request a refund

Email us with:

  • The email used at checkout
  • Your license key (if you have it)
  • Approximate purchase date
  • A short note that you want a refund

Contact: iclearerglobal@gmail.com

We aim to respond within a few business days.

3. Subscriptions

  • Cancel anytime: cancelling stops future renewals. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • Within 14 days of a charge: you may request a refund of that charge under this policy.
  • After 14 days: the period already paid is generally non-refundable, but you can still cancel to avoid the next renewal.

You can also manage billing through the receipt or account emails Paddle sends after purchase.

4. One-time purchases

One-time (version 1) purchases are refundable within 14 days of purchase under the same guarantee. After 14 days, one-time purchases are generally non-refundable.

5. Free plan

The free plan does not involve payment, so refunds do not apply.

6. Chargebacks

Please contact us before opening a chargeback. We can usually resolve billing issues faster by email. Abuse of chargebacks may result in license suspension.

7. Related policies

See also our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
